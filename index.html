What this is

We installed Mozart AI, went through onboarding to the paywall, closed it, and compared what we saw with the report.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and every year we publish what their paywalls do in State of Paywalls. We held Mozart's onboarding paywall up against that data: the plans and which one is picked for you, the price framing, the close button, and what happens when someone says no.

What follows is four ideas, each tied to a number from the report and each rebuilt as a clickable screen. The prototype uses your design, not ours. Benchmarks are for all categories in the US, your largest App Store market; the report has no Music category.

We did not redesign your paywall

Every screen in the prototype is yours: the singer photo with the before-and-after avatar, the three plan cards, the magenta selection, the blue Continue button and your Welcome Bonus. Only what is picked, shown and asked changed.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes first, cosmetics last: in the report, changes to plans and trials win A/B tests more often than visual or copy changes. Each idea links straight to its screen in the prototype.

01

Open the onboarding paywall on 1 Year

The paywall every new user sees opens on 1 Week at €5.99. Your in-app PRO paywall already opens on 1 Year at €49.99. Same plans and prices, different starting point, on the screen that sees the most new users.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · 90% of buyers keep a pre-selected weekly plan, 46% a pre-selected annual one, with no drop in conversion. See the screen 90% → 46%buyers who keep the
pre-selected plan · report finding
02

Ask once more when they close

Tapping X goes straight to the Welcome Bonus and its free credits. Nobody hears about the annual plan again. Put one sheet in between: 1 Year with a 7-day free trial, then the bonus if they still say no.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · only 4% of apps show a second paywall; it converts at 0.88× the first and beats it in 45% of apps. See the screen ARPU +10–15%Based on Adapty data
03

Put your 4.3 stars on the paywall

Mozart has 4.3 stars from 32K ratings on the US App Store, and the “#1 AI Music App” laurels open the app. Neither appears on the paywall, the screen where people decide.

📊State of Paywalls 2026 · only 27% of top paywalls show social proof. See the screen CR +10–30%Observed in winning A/B tests
· State of Paywalls 2026
04

Hold the close button for 3 seconds

The X is live the moment the paywall appears, so a quick tap skips the plans before anyone has read them. A short ring that fills for 3 seconds keeps the paywall soft, it just asks for a look first.
